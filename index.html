    FROM DOCUMENT TO GAME NIGHT
    
      
    Import your questions
    
      
    
        Turn existing questions and marked answers into an editable quiz.
        Nothing is saved until you review it.
      
    
      
    
        
        
    
          Formatting is a hint, not proof. Always review inferred answers. For
          multiple answers, use “Answers: A, C”.
        
    
        
        
    
          Text PDF (up to 50 pages), Word .docx or UTF-8 .txt · maximum 5 MB. No
          scans or photographs yet. Use explicit question numbers and A)–F)
          labels; automatic Word list numbering may not survive extraction.
        
    
        
        
    
          See the supported format
          
    Title: Community quiz
Round 1
1. What is the capital of France?
A) Rome
B) Paris
Answer: B

2. The Earth orbits the Sun. (True/False)
Answer: True

3. Finish the phrase: Better ____.
Answer: together
    
          
    
            Alternatively, add an “Answer key:” section with lines such as “1.
            B”. Missing or conflicting answers are left for you to select—not
            guessed.
          
    
        
    
        
    
          Processed on your Quizzes server. No paid AI or external document
          service. Files are not retained; only the quiz you choose to save is
          stored.
